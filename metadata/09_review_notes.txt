This app's primary feature is a lock screen widget that displays real-time NOAA buoy data.

To test the widget:
1. Open the app and note the default buoy station (44065). You can change this to any valid NOAA station ID.
2. Close the app.
3. To customize your lock screen, either long press the lock screen and tap "Customize", or go to Settings > Wallpaper > Customize.
4. Tap "Add Widgets", search for "BuoyData", and add the circular widget.
5. The widget will display live swell data (wave height, swell height, period, and direction) for the selected buoy station.

The app requires an internet connection to fetch data from NOAA's National Data Buoy Center.
