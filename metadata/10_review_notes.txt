This app's primary feature is a lock screen widget that displays real-time NOAA buoy data.

To test:
1. Open the app. On Favorites, add a buoy by ID (e.g. 44065) or use the Map to add one.
2. Confirm readings appear on Favorites and on the Map for selected stations.
3. Close the app. To customize your lock screen, either long press the lock screen and tap "Customize", or go to Settings > Wallpaper > Customize.
4. Tap "Add Widgets", search for "Buoys", and add the circular widget.
5. The widget will display live swell data (wave height, swell height, period, and direction) for the first buoy in the Favorites tab.

The app requires an internet connection to fetch data from NOAA's National Data Buoy Center.
