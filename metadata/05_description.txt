BuoyData puts live ocean conditions on your lock screen with a compact circular widget. Check the swell at a glance, without unlocking your phone.

Get real-time wave height, swell height, swell period, and swell direction from any NOAA buoy station, updated automatically throughout the day.

Perfect for surfers, anglers, boaters, and anyone who needs to real-time ocean conditions.

FEATURES

- Lock screen widget showing live swell data in a compact format
- Displays significant wave height, swell height, swell period, and swell direction
- Data sourced directly from NOAA's National Data Buoy Center (NDBC)
- Choose from over 1,400 buoy stations across the US coastline

GETTING STARTED

1. Open BuoyData and enter your preferred NOAA buoy station ID (find yours at ndbc.noaa.gov)
2. To customize your lock screen, either long press the lock screen and tap "Customize", or go to Settings > Wallpaper > Customize
3. Tap "Add Widgets" and search for BuoyData
4. Select the widget — that's it!

Your buoy data is now always visible on your lock screen.
